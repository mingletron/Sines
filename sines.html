TEK SINES — Additive Synthesis Oscilloscope

Build sounds by stacking sine waves and watch the resulting waveform and its frequency spectrum update live.

Getting Started

Click a Preset (Sine, Sawtooth, Square, Triangle) to load a classic waveform, then press Space or ▶ Play to hear it. Add more waves with + Add Wave or +.

Oscilloscope Terminology

Graticule — the grid lines on the screen. Each division represents a fixed amount of time (horizontal) or voltage (vertical).

Time/Div — shown in the top-left of the waveform screen. Tells you how many milliseconds each horizontal grid division represents.

V/Div — shown in the bottom-left. Tells you the amplitude scale per vertical division.

Trigger — synchronises the waveform display so the trace starts at the same point in each cycle, giving a stable image. Without triggering, the trace drifts.

FFT — Fast Fourier Transform. The mathematical operation that converts the time-domain waveform into its frequency spectrum, showing which frequencies are present and how strong they are.

Persistence — the fading trail on the waveform screen, simulating a phosphor CRT where previous traces linger before fading.
